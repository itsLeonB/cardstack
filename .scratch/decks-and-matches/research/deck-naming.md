# Deck naming and archetype terms

Checked on 2026-11-25. Parent checked the worker's sources and corrected its date handling and unverified examples below.

## Verified examples

- Limitless publishes a page titled “Ceruledge - Deck Overview,” with “Variant,” “Core Cards,” and multiple players' individual deck lists. This verifies Ceruledge as a published deck-family label, not a unique personal deck or an exact list. Source directly fetched: https://limitlesstcg.com/decks/299.
- TCGenius publishes “Dragapult Blaziken deck list,” describes multiple “Dragapult Blaziken lists,” and gives one specific player's list separately. Its example list contains Dragapult ex and Blaziken ex as well as other Pokémon. This verifies the publisher's use of the combined name, not an official taxonomy or Indonesian legality. Source directly fetched: https://tcgenius.gg/pokemon/tournaments/dragapult-blaziken.
- Pokémon's official Palkia strategy article calls Origin Forme Palkia VSTAR “our main attacker” and describes “the now well-established Inteleon engine.” These are roles within one deck, not evidence that each constituent Pokémon is a separate archetype. Source directly fetched: https://www.pokemon.com/us/strategy/origin-forme-palkia-vstar-deck-strategy.

## What the evidence does not settle

The examples support compact deck names built from one or more prominent Pokémon. They do not establish a universal term for each component or a maximum of two components. This small source set cannot prove that no local community uses archetype differently.

A personal Deck, its descriptive name or family label, its key Pokémon and an exact card list remain distinct possible concepts. The user must choose which matter to this release. Exact lists remain outside the confirmed MVP scope.

## Open product decisions

- Should a Deck have one free-text descriptive name, or structured key-Pokémon choices that produce its name?
- Should the app impose a maximum of two choices, or treat one or two as common examples only?
- Can two of the user's Decks share the same descriptive name, perhaps with an optional nickname?
- Who maintains any reusable naming choices, and what happens for unknown opponent decks?
