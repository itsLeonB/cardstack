# Match results: rules research

Checked on 2026-11-25. Scope: private Indonesian Standard records, not tournament administration.

## Verified regional source

The parent directly fetched and read the Indonesian Floor Rules, version Asia 1.2, last updated 13 March 2025: https://asia.pokemon-card.com/id/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/2025/03/IDN_Pokemon-Trading-Card-Game-Floor-Rules-Ver.Asia-1.2.pdf.

- Section 9, pages 9–10: “Permainan selesai dan menang kalah ditetapkan dengan mengikuti regulasi yang ditetapkan pada tiap turnamen.” Game endings and win/loss decisions follow the regulations for each tournament. The same section permits the Head Judge and organizer to determine the outcome when it is difficult to decide.
- Section 10-1, page 10: “Sebelum pertandingan berakhir atau ada keputusan dari Judge mengenai menang kalah, maka pemain dapat menyerah kalah atau menjadikan pertandingan seri berdasarkan persetujuan kedua pemain.” This permits concessions and agreed draws under that section's conditions; it does not require a played game to exist for every match result.
- Section 5-2-2, pages 6–7: “Pastikan untuk mengecek pengumuman pertandingan dan peraturan berpartisipasi turnamen yang diikuti.” Event announcements and participation rules can specify their own operating policy.
- Section 2-3, page 4 prohibits “Melakukan panggilan telepon atau menggunakan ponsel saat sedang bertanding” unless event staff expressly permit it. Live record entry must not imply permission to use a phone during sanctioned play.
- Section 6-1-1, page 8 disallows imitation cards, including photocopied and handmade substitutes, except for judge-issued replacements under its stated conditions. Future casual Proxy support must not imply tournament legality.

## Implications for product decisions

A personal tracker can suggest normal match results from game records while allowing the user to record an actual concession, agreed draw or judge result without making up games. This is a proposed product choice, not a complete tournament rule engine.

Keep unfinished games distinct from drawn games. Whether the product supports a played game's draw, an unfinished final game, a match-level result override, a bye or an unplayed match is for the interview to settle.
