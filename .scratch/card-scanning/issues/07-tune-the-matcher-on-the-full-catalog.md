This ticket produces a script and a report; it ships no product behaviour.

**Current behavior:**
Ticket 01's accuracy and its provisional `confident` rule come from a 199-Card gallery, which is far easier than the real catalog of about 12,000 Cards. Neither the dimension nor the tie rate between reprints has been measured against the full catalog.

**Desired behavior:**
A re-runnable offline script and a short report, run against the full embedded catalog with the model ticket 01 chose and the real phone photos:
- **Accuracy on the full catalog:** top-1 and top-5 over the photo set, to be compared with ticket 01's `MA6` numbers. The 90 and 98 percent bar is checked here against a realistic search space; if the chosen model misses it, the report says so and recommends the fallback (self-hosted service or the OCR hybrid) before ticket 06 starts.
- **Vector dimension:** accuracy and index cost at each Matryoshka size the chosen model offers, with a recommended dimension at or below the pgvector index limit of 2000.
- **`confident` rule:** the score distribution of correct versus incorrect top-1 matches over the full catalog, and a concrete top-score threshold plus runner-up margin tuned so that a confident result is almost never wrong, accepting that non-confident results are common. Precision and coverage at the recommended values are stated.
- **Reprint ties:** how often two catalog Cards tie because the same artwork is reprinted in more than one Expansion Set, estimated with the method in the research notes since the catalog has no field linking reprints. A tie must be non-confident, and the report confirms the recommended rule makes it so.
- The report states the final values ticket 06 should use as its initial configuration.
