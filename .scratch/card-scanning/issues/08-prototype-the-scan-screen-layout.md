## Agent Brief

**Category:** research (UI prototype)
**Summary:** Decide how the scan screen is laid out once the Draft Addition tray holds many Scans. Build a throwaway prototype with three layouts on the scan route, switchable from a floating bar, so the user can feel each one with fake data before ticket 09 builds the real thing. The deliverable is a verdict, not shipped code.

**Current behavior:**
The scan screen is the camera viewfinder and Capture button, then the tray of the draft below them, then the "Review and add" button. A tray of 30 or more rows pushes the viewfinder off screen, so after checking a row the user has to scroll back up to scan the next card.

**The question to answer:**
With 30 or more Scans in the tray, which layout keeps the scan loop (aim, tap, glance at the newest row) in one view, and keeps "Review and add" reachable, on a phone and on a desktop?

**Variants to build** (all on one route, chosen by a URL search param, per the prototype skill):
- **(a) Pinned.** The viewfinder and Capture button stay pinned at the top. The tray scrolls underneath. A pinned bottom bar holds "Review and add" and a "N still matching" note. The viewfinder may shrink a little while pinned.
- **(b) Normal flow.** The viewfinder, then the tray, then "Review and add", as today.
- **(c) Sheet.** A full-screen viewfinder, with the tray as a collapsible bottom sheet over it. The sheet's collapsed state shows the count and the newest row.

**Row anatomy, the same in every variant:**
- Left: the card image once the Scan resolves. While the Scan is queued or loading, the user's own captured photo as a thumbnail with a spinner overlaid. When it failed, the same thumbnail with a red cross overlaid.
- Middle: the card name, Expansion Set and card number.
- Right: quantity raise and lower, a dropdown of the other candidates (only when the match was not confident), and Remove. The newest row is on top.

| State | Left slot | Right side |
| --- | --- | --- |
| queued | own photo, spinner | Remove |
| loading | own photo, spinner | Remove |
| failed | own photo, red cross | Retry, Remove |
| resolved, confident | card image | quantity, Remove |
| resolved, not confident | card image | quantity, dropdown, Remove |

"Review and add" is disabled while any Scan is queued or loading, and the pinned or sheet variants show why.

**Fake data and behavior:**
- A fake frame source and timers stand in for the camera and the match endpoint. Nothing is wired to the real API, and there is no persistence.
- Up to 3 simulated matches run at once and further captures wait as queued rows. Some matches resolve as confident, some as not confident with five candidates, and one fails.
- Each variant must let the user reach all five row states and 30 or more rows.

**Key interfaces:**

## Answer

**Verdict: layout (c), the sheet.** The user tried the variants and chose a full-screen viewfinder with the Draft Addition as a collapsible bottom sheet over it. Ticket 09 implements it.

What (c) does in the prototype, and what ticket 09 keeps:
- The viewfinder fills the screen below the site header, and Capture sits just above the sheet, so aiming, tapping and the newest row stay in one view at any tray size.
- The collapsed sheet is one header line plus the newest row. The header holds the toggle with the Scan count, the "N still matching" note, and "Review and add", so the button is reachable at every tray size and the disabled state explains itself.
- Expanding the sheet gives a scrollable list of every Scan, newest first, with the quantity, dropdown and Remove controls.
- On a desktop the viewfinder and sheet sit in a phone-width column (about 28rem) centred on the screen.

Not covered by the prototype, left to ticket 09: the page header and breadcrumbs (the full-screen viewfinder hides them, so a way back to the Collection is needed), the camera denied and unavailable states, "Choose a photo", and "Discard draft".
