# 36: Collection entries and Master Inventory on infinite scroll

**Category:** enhancement
**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Blocked by:** 33, 34

Findings: `.scratch/cardstack-mvp/research/virtualized-infinite-scroll.md` (sections 1.3, 4.2, 5 and 8.4). Related tickets: 20, 22, 23, 29.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Move the Collection detail page and the Master Inventory view to the infinite, virtualized card grid, without breaking the +/- quantity controls and their debounced saves.

**Current behavior:**
Both lists use numbered pages. `useQuantityBatch` prunes pending edits against the loaded list; once pages append, that pruning shows stale quantities again after a save (reproduced in the research prototype). The Collection entries query uses `gcTime: 0`, and focus refetch rules come from ticket 29.

**Desired behavior:**
- Both lists load more on scroll through the shared grid and data hook, at page size 100.
- A saved quantity change patches the cached pages in place (a cache update across all loaded pages) from the bulk update result, so a saved value never flips back to a stale one and a refetch is not needed. A regression test reproduces the research scenario (several pages loaded, edit, save, append another page).
- Every invalidation that must reach these lists (for example the Master Inventory invalidation and any entries-list invalidation by collection) targets the infinite query keys, which start with `'infinite'`, using the generated Infinite key helpers.
- Ticket 29's rules are kept: window-focus refetch is skipped while edits are pending or being saved. The scroll trigger is also guarded while a fetch is in flight. Focus refetch re-requests all loaded pages in sequence; record whether that cost is acceptable and the `gcTime` decision (including its effect on back-navigation restoration) in the ticket.
- A card reduced to 0 behaves per ticket 29's agreed rule; the one-skipped-card-after-zeroing case noted in the research is either mitigated or recorded as accepted.

**Key interfaces:**
- The Collection entries and Master Inventory queries and their route components.
- `useQuantityBatch` and its saved-results callback, and the generated bulk update endpoint.
- The shared grid from tickets 33 and 34.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Both lists scroll-load all entries with no duplicates
- [ ] Editing a quantity on a card on page 3 and saving keeps the saved value after further pages load and after a focus refetch
- [ ] No invalidation silently misses an infinite list (test or documented check per call site)
- [ ] Focus refetch is skipped with pending edits and runs otherwise
- [ ] Tests updated and a Playwright e2e for editing a quantity after scrolling
- [ ] Both verification scripts pass

**Out of scope:**
- The catalog's quantities lookup (ticket 37)
- Backend changes, cursor pagination
