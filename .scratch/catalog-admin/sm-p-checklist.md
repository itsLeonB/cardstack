# SM-P checklist

Checklist of the Indonesian promo set `SM-P` ("Kartu Promo Matahari & Bulan") to enter cards from, one row per collector number. Ticket: `.scratch/catalog-admin/issues/07-sm-p-checklist-from-archived-bulbapedia-list.md`.

Attribution: collector numbers, English names and categories are facts taken from Bulbapedia's "SM-P Promotional cards (ITCG)" page, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 2.5 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.5/), so Cardstack must stay non-commercial. Source: Wayback Machine snapshot dated 2025-07-20, https://web.archive.org/web/20250720130520/https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/SM-P_Promotional_cards_(ITCG). The page's Promotion text and per-card prose are not copied.

Notes:

- English names are used until corrected from the physical card.
- Rarity is "Tanpa Tanda" for all cards.
- Pokémon cards carry no HP, types or attacks; Energi cards have an empty Tag.
- Category and Tag come from the source's type code: blank is Pokémon, `I`, `Su`, `St` are Trainer with Tag Item, Supporter, Stadium, and `E` is Energi.
- Rows in the Flag column share one source row between several prints; decide how to enter them when you hold the cards.
